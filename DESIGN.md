# Home Movies visual design

Approved direction: A, Slate (October 11, 2026).

Use crisp white surfaces on a pale gray background, navy actions, system sans-serif typography, fine borders, and restrained rounded corners. The shared stylesheet is `python_app/static/slate.css`. Templates share the shell, login fields, flash messages, and Video.js markup.

Keep the current section links, movie links, password/QR authentication, five-second QR polling, logout, player controls, and sharing flow. Do not add search, thumbnails, favorites, or catalog metadata as part of this visual refresh.

The login methods share a divided card on desktop and stack at 650px or below. Keep the QR image square, bounded, centered, and on white with padding. Collection labels and movie titles must wrap without horizontal overflow. Shared playback uses the same visual shell without authenticated navigation.

Use existing server-provided titles, sections, and URLs. The sample names in the original comps are not application content.
